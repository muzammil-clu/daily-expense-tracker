Privacy Policy - Expense Tracker

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Daily Expense Tracker ("the app") does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal information.

  • No account, no permissions: the app requests no device permissions.
  • Local storage only: expenses, budget, currency and theme stay on your device. You can delete them in the app (Settings, then Delete all data) or by uninstalling the app.
  • Offline: no internet use, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Not financial advice: the app is a personal record-keeping tool. It provides no banking, payment or lending services.
  • Children: the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
  • Changes: updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
